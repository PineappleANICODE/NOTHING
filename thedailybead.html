BREAKING NEWS: CONVICT FROM THE CUDDIOBOROUGH CASE HAS DISSAPEARED

Murder or accident? Locals shocked by tragic death of beloved townsmember

Uploaded 24 hours ago

This morning, Melek Gilmore was killed in Cuddioborough by a local who's name has not yet been revealed by police. Melek was known in the local area for being quite quiet and introverted, but also very kind and generous. His family say "he was an avid bookworm who loved imagining different worlds. He will be missed by all who got to know him."
Before the incident , witnesses report that the perpetrator had been acting strange all morning, "as if a pressure was building inside of them". The perpetrator was then in a car by themselves, when dash cam footage shows them seemingly speed up in order to hit Melek, who was crossing about 20 meters ahead. After the attack, nearby police rushed to the scene, where they report that the perpetrator had collapsed to the ground, gagging and visibly pale. They were both rushed to the hospital, where Melek unfortunatley passed away only a couple of hours later. The perpetrator is withought physical injury, but is reportedly very ill and unable to walk.
